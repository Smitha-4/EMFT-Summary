Time-Varying Fields & Waveguides Summary

Maxwell's equations, electromagnetic waves, transmission lines, and waveguide propagation parameters.

Maxwell's Equations for Time-Varying Fields

Law / Name Differential (Point) Form Integral Form Physical Significance
Faraday's Law $$\nabla \times \mathbf{E} = -\frac{\partial \mathbf{B}}{\partial t}$$ $$\oint_C \mathbf{E} \cdot d\mathbf{l} = -\frac{\partial}{\partial t} \int_S \mathbf{B} \cdot d\mathbf{s}$$ Time-varying magnetic field induces an electric field.
Ampere-Maxwell Law $$\nabla \times \mathbf{H} = \mathbf{J} + \frac{\partial \mathbf{D}}{\partial t}$$ $$\oint_C \mathbf{H} \cdot d\mathbf{l} = \int_S \left(\mathbf{J} + \frac{\partial \mathbf{D}}{\partial t}\right) \cdot d\mathbf{s}$$ Magnetic fields are produced by conduction current and displacement current.
Gauss's Law for Electricity $$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{D} = \rho_v$$ $$\oint_S \mathbf{D} \cdot d\mathbf{s} = Q_{\text{enc}}$$ Electric flux diverges from volume charge sources.
Gauss's Law for Magnetism $$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0$$ $$\oint_S \mathbf{B} \cdot d\mathbf{s} = 0$$ Absence of isolated magnetic charges (monopoles).

Core Time-Varying & Wave Quantities, Definitions & Units

Quantity Symbol Definition SI Unit
Propagation Constant $\gamma$ Complex measure of phase shift and attenuation per unit length ($\alpha + j\beta$). per meter ($\text{m}^{-1}$)
Attenuation Constant $\alpha$ Rate of amplitude decay of an electromagnetic wave along the direction of propagation. Np per meter ($\text{Np/m}$)
Phase Constant $\beta$ Phase change per unit length along the wave propagation path. radians per meter ($\text{rad/m}$)
Intrinsic Impedance $\eta$ Ratio of electric field intensity to magnetic field intensity in a medium ($\sqrt{\frac{j\omega\mu}{\sigma + j\omega\epsilon}}$). Ohms ($\Omega$)
Poynting Vector $\mathbf{S}$ Directional energy flux density of an electromagnetic field ($\mathbf{E} \times \mathbf{H}$). Watts per square meter ($\text{W/m}^2$)
Characteristic Impedance $Z_0$ Ratio of voltage to current waves in a transmission line ($\sqrt{\frac{R+j\omega L}{G+j\omega C}}$). Ohms ($\Omega$)
Reflection Coefficient $\Gamma$ or $\rho$ Ratio of reflected wave amplitude to incident wave amplitude. Dimensionless (complex)
Standing Wave Ratio $\text{SWR}$ or $S$ Ratio of maximum to minimum voltage amplitudes on a transmission line. Dimensionless ($\ge 1$)

Electromagnetic Waves Across Media

Lossless Dielectric ($\sigma = 0$)

Parameters: $\alpha = 0, \beta = \omega\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}$

$\eta = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{\epsilon}}, \quad v = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}}$

Good Conductor ($\sigma \gg \omega\epsilon$)

Parameters: Skin depth $\delta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi f \mu \sigma}}$

$\alpha = \beta = \sqrt{\pi f \mu \sigma}, \quad \eta = (1+j)\sqrt{\frac{\pi f \mu}{\sigma}}$

Free Space ($\sigma=0, \epsilon=\epsilon_0, \mu=\mu_0$)

Parameters: Speed of light $c = 3 \times 10^8$ m/s

$\eta_0 = \sqrt{\frac{\mu_0}{\epsilon_0}} \approx 377\,\Omega$

Transmission Lines & Rectangular Waveguides

Input Impedance ($Z_{\text{in}}$)

Line Length: $l$ with Load $Z_L

$Z_{\text{in}} = Z_0 \frac{Z_L + j Z_0 \tan(\beta l)}{Z_0 + j Z_L \tan(\beta l)}$

Cutoff Frequency ($f_c$)

Rectangular Guide: Dimensions $a \times b$ ($m, n$ modes)

$f_c = \frac{v}{2}\sqrt{\left(\frac{m}{a}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{n}{b}\right)^2}$

Guide Wavelength ($\lambda_g$)

Waveguide Parameter

$\lambda_g = \frac{\lambda}{\sqrt{1 - (f_c/f)^2}}$
