Electrostatics Summary

Core theorems, field quantities, and charge distributions across different coordinate systems.

Relationships Among Electromagnetic Quantities in Simple Terms

Electric Field Intensity ($\mathbf{E}$)

Electric force experienced per unit positive test charge: $\mathbf{E} = \frac{\mathbf{F}}{Q}$[cite: 1].

Electric Flux Density ($\mathbf{D}$)

Total electric flux per unit area, directly proportional to the electric field in a medium: $\mathbf{D} = \varepsilon \mathbf{E}$[cite: 1].

Potential Difference ($V$)

Work done in moving a unit positive charge between two points against the electric field: $V = -\int \mathbf{E} \cdot d\mathbf{l}$[cite: 1].

Current Density ($\mathbf{J}$)

Electric current flowing per unit cross-sectional area: $\mathbf{J} = \frac{I}{A} = \rho_v \mathbf{v}$[cite: 1].

Fundamental Theorems of Electrostatics

1. Coulomb's Law

Defines force and field intensity generated by static point charges.

2. Gauss's Law (Integral Form)

$$\oint \mathbf{D} \cdot d\mathbf{s} = Q_{\text{enclosed}}$$

3. Gauss's Law (Point Form)

$$\nabla \cdot \mathbf{D} = \rho_v$$

4. Conservative Property

$$\nabla \times \mathbf{E} = 0 \implies \mathbf{E} = -\nabla V$$

Core Electrostatic Quantities, Definitions & Units

Quantity Symbol Definition SI Unit
Electric Force $\mathbf{F}$ The attractive or repulsive electrostatic force experienced by a charge in an electric field. Newtons ($\text{N}$)
Electric Field Intensity $\mathbf{E}$ The electrostatic force experienced per unit test charge. Volts per meter ($\text{V/m}$) or ($\text{N/C}$)
Electric Flux $\Psi$ The total measure of electric field lines passing through a given surface. Coulombs ($\text{C}$)
Electric Flux Density $\mathbf{D}$ A measure of the strength of electric flux per unit area normal to the field. Coulombs per square meter ($\text{C/m}^2$)
Electric Potential $V$ The work done per unit positive charge in bringing it from infinity to a point in the field. Volts ($\text{V}$)
Electric Charge $Q$ The fundamental property of matter responsible for electrostatic interactions. Coulombs ($\text{C}$)
Volume Charge Density $\rho_v$ The amount of charge distributed continuously per unit volume. Coulombs per cubic meter ($\text{C/m}^3$)
Surface Charge Density $\sigma$ The amount of charge distributed continuously per unit surface area. Coulombs per square meter ($\text{C/m}^2$)
Line Charge Density $\lambda$ The amount of charge distributed continuously per unit length. Coulombs per meter ($\text{C/m}$)

Electric Force ($\mathbf{F}$) Across Geometries & Systems

Point Charge

System: Spherical $(r, \theta, \phi)$

$\mathbf{F} = \frac{qQ}{4\pi\varepsilon r^2} \mathbf{a}_r$

Infinite Line Charge

System: Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

$\mathbf{F} = \frac{q\lambda}{2\pi\varepsilon \rho} \mathbf{a}_\rho$

Infinite Sheet of Charge

System: Cartesian $(x, y, z)$

$\mathbf{F} = \frac{q\sigma}{2\varepsilon} \mathbf{a}_n$

Uniform Charged Ring

System: Cylindrical/Cartesian Axis

$\mathbf{F}_z = \frac{qQ z}{4\pi\varepsilon (R^2 + z^2)^{3/2}} \mathbf{a}_z$

Circular Disk (Axis)

System: Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

$\mathbf{F}_z = \frac{q\sigma}{2\varepsilon} \left(1 - \frac{z}{\sqrt{z^2 + R^2}}\right) \mathbf{a}_z$

Cylindrical Shell / Wire

System: Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

Inside ($\rho < a$): $0$
Outside ($\rho \ge a$): $\frac{q a\sigma}{\varepsilon \rho} \mathbf{a}_\rho$

Solid Sphere / Spherical Shell

System: Spherical $(r, \theta, \phi)$

Inside ($r < R$): $\mathbf{F} = \frac{q\rho_v r}{3\varepsilon} \mathbf{a}_r$  |  Outside ($r \ge R$): $\mathbf{F} = \frac{qQ}{4\pi\varepsilon r^2} \mathbf{a}_r$

Electric Field Intensity Formulas for Various Charge Distributions & Cases

Point & Discrete Charges

Point Charge: $\mathbf{E} = \frac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^2}\mathbf{a}_r$

Discrete System: $\mathbf{E} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{Q_i(\mathbf{r} - \mathbf{r}_i)}{|\mathbf{r} - \mathbf{r}_i|^3}$

Line & Ring Charges

Infinite Line Charge ($\rho_L$): $\mathbf{E} = \frac{\rho_L}{2\pi\varepsilon_0 \rho}\mathbf{a}_\rho$

Charged Circular Ring: $\mathbf{E} = \frac{Qz}{4\pi\varepsilon_0(z^2 + a^2)^{3/2}}\mathbf{a}_z$

Sheet & Volume Distributions

Infinite Sheet ($\rho_s$): $\mathbf{E} = \frac{\rho_s}{2\varepsilon_0}\mathbf{a}_n$

Uniform Sphere ($r \le R$): $\mathbf{E} = \frac{\rho_v r}{3\varepsilon_0}\mathbf{a}_r$
($r > R$): $\mathbf{E} = \frac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^2}\mathbf{a}_r$

Electric Flux ($\Psi$) Across Geometries & Systems

Point Charge

System: Spherical $(r, \theta, \phi)$

$\Psi = Q$

Infinite Line Charge

System: Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

$\Psi_{\text{enclosed}} = \lambda L$ (per length $L$)

Infinite Sheet of Charge

System: Cartesian $(x, y, z)$

$\Psi = \sigma S$ (for area $S$)

Uniform Charged Ring

System: Cylindrical/Cartesian Axis

$\Psi = Q$

Circular Disk (Axis)

System: Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

$\Psi = Q = \pi R^2 \sigma$

Cylindrical Shell / Wire

System: Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

$\Psi = 2\pi a L \sigma$

Solid Sphere / Spherical Shell

System: Spherical $(r, \theta, \phi)$

$\Psi = Q_{\text{enclosed}}$ (equals $Q$ for full sphere)

Electric Flux Density ($\mathbf{D}$) Across Geometries & Systems

Point Charge

System: Spherical $(r, \theta, \phi)$

$\mathbf{D} = \frac{Q}{4\pi r^2} \mathbf{a}_r$

Infinite Line Charge

System: Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

$\mathbf{D} = \frac{\lambda}{2\pi \rho} \mathbf{a}_\rho$

Infinite Sheet of Charge

System: Cartesian $(x, y, z)$

$\mathbf{D} = \frac{\sigma}{2} \mathbf{a}_n$

Uniform Charged Ring

System: Cylindrical/Cartesian Axis

$\mathbf{D}_z = \frac{Q z}{4\pi (R^2 + z^2)^{3/2}} \mathbf{a}_z$

Circular Disk (Axis)

System: Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

$\mathbf{D}_z = \frac{\sigma}{2} \left(1 - \frac{z}{\sqrt{z^2 + R^2}}\right) \mathbf{a}_z$

Cylindrical Shell / Wire

System: Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

Inside ($\rho < a$): $0$
Outside ($\rho \ge a$): $\frac{a\sigma}{\rho} \mathbf{a}_\rho$

Solid Sphere / Spherical Shell

System: Spherical $(r, \theta, \phi)$

Inside ($r < R$): $\mathbf{D} = \frac{\rho_v r}{3} \mathbf{a}_r$  |  Outside ($r \ge R$): $\mathbf{D} = \frac{Q}{4\pi r^2} \mathbf{a}_r$

Electric Potential ($V$) Across Geometries & Systems

Point Charge

System: Spherical $(r, \theta, \phi)$

$V = \frac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon r}$

Infinite Line Charge

System: Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

$V = -\frac{\lambda}{2\pi\varepsilon} \ln\left(\frac{\rho}{\rho_0}\right)$

Infinite Sheet of Charge

System: Cartesian $(x, y, z)$

$V = -\frac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon} z$

Uniform Charged Ring

System: Cylindrical/Cartesian Axis

$V = \frac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon \sqrt{R^2 + z^2}}$

Circular Disk (Axis)

System: Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

$V = \frac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon}\left(\sqrt{z^2 + R^2} - z\right)$

Cylindrical Shell / Wire

System: Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

Outside ($\rho \ge a$): $\frac{a\sigma}{\varepsilon}\ln\left(\frac{b}{\rho}\right)$

Solid Sphere / Spherical Shell

System: Spherical $(r, \theta, \phi)$

Inside ($r < R$): $V = \frac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon R}\left(1 - \frac{r^2}{3R^2}\right)$  |  Outside ($r \ge R$): $V = \frac{Q}{4\pi\varepsilon r}$

Charge ($Q$) Across Geometries & Systems

Point Charge

System: Spherical $(r, \theta, \phi)$

$Q = \text{Discrete Charge } (q)$

Infinite Line Charge

System: Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

$Q = \int \lambda \, dl = \lambda L$

Infinite Sheet of Charge

System: Cartesian $(x, y, z)$

$Q = \int \sigma \, ds = \sigma S$

Uniform Charged Ring

System: Cylindrical/Cartesian Axis

$Q = \oint \lambda \, dl = 2\pi R \lambda$

Circular Disk (Axis)

System: Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

$Q = \int_0^{2\pi} \int_0^R \sigma \rho \, d\rho \, d\phi = \pi R^2 \sigma$

Cylindrical Shell / Wire

System: Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

$Q = \int \sigma \, ds = 2\pi a L \sigma$

Solid Sphere / Spherical Shell

System: Spherical $(r, \theta, \phi)$

$Q = \int \rho_v \, dv = \frac{4}{3}\pi R^3 \rho_v$ (Solid)  |  $Q = 4\pi R^2 \sigma$ (Shell)

Charge Density ($\rho_v, \sigma, \lambda$) Across Geometries & Systems

Point Charge

System: Spherical $(r, \theta, \phi)$

$\rho_v = Q \, \delta(r)$ (Dirac Delta)

Infinite Line Charge

System: Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

$\lambda = \frac{dQ}{dl}$ ($\text{C/m}$)

Infinite Sheet of Charge

System: Cartesian $(x, y, z)$

$\sigma = \frac{dQ}{ds}$ ($\text{C/m}^2$)

Uniform Charged Ring

System: Cylindrical/Cartesian Axis

$\lambda = \frac{Q}{2\pi R}$ ($\text{C/m}$)

Circular Disk (Axis)

System: Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

$\sigma = \frac{Q}{\pi R^2}$ ($\text{C/m}^2$)

Cylindrical Shell / Wire

System: Cylindrical $(\rho, \phi, z)$

$\sigma = \frac{Q}{2\pi a L}$ ($\text{C/m}^2$)

Solid Sphere / Spherical Shell

System: Spherical $(r, \theta, \phi)$

$\rho_v = \frac{3Q}{4\pi R^3}$ ($\text{C/m}^3$) (Solid)  |  $\sigma = \frac{Q}{4\pi R^2}$ ($\text{C/m}^2$) (Shell)
